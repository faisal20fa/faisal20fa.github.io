Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Pulse is a private app used only by its owner to view their own health data.

What data Pulse reads

With your permission, Pulse reads (read-only) your Google Health data: sleep, heart rate and other health measurements, activity and fitness, and basic profile data. Pulse never writes or changes your data.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show charts and summaries to you inside the app. It is stored only on your own devices.

Sharing

Pulse does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. It does not use the data for advertising, and it does not use it to train AI models.

Pulse's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove Pulse's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app removes all data stored on your device.
